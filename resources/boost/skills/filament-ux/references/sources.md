## Known age caveats

- NN/g dashboards-preattentive, search-visible-and-simple, search-no-results, checkboxes-vs-radio-buttons, date-input and password-creation are 8–9 years old; every rule citing them also has a newer source.
- The claim that most users can't use Boolean search syntax rests only on NN/g (2001), so QueryBuilder-for-trained-users is tagged **P**.
